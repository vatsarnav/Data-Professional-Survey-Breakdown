Data Professional Survey Breakdown

Overview

This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze survey responses from data professionals.

The dashboard explores career-related trends and professional experiences across areas such as job roles, salary, programming language preferences, location, work-life balance, job satisfaction, and the difficulty of entering the data field.

Dashboard

The report is presented as a single interactive Power BI page titled Data Professional Survey Breakdown.

Key Visualizations

Average Salary by Job Title — compares average salary across different data-related job titles.

Favorite Programming Language — shows programming language preferences across respondents and job roles.

Country of Survey Takers — visualizes the geographic distribution of survey respondents.

Happiness with Work Life Balance — measures respondents' satisfaction with their work-life balance.

Happiness Index with relation to Salary — examines satisfaction in relation to salary.

Difficulty to break into Data — shows how respondents describe the difficulty of entering the data field.

Summary Cards — provides high-level survey metrics, including respondent and age-related measures.

Data Analysis

The dashboard uses survey responses to explore questions such as:

How does average salary vary across data-related job titles?

Which programming languages are most commonly preferred by respondents?

Where are survey respondents located?

How satisfied are data professionals with their work-life balance?

How does salary relate to reported satisfaction?

How difficult do respondents find it to break into the data field?
